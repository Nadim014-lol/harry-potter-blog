WELCOME TO THE BLOG

Five journeys.
One magical world.

Harry Potter is one of the most popular fantasy book series. This blog features reviews of five books from the series, focusing on their stories, characters, and my personal thoughts.

BOOK REVIEWS

Five Books

01

BOOK ONE

Harry Potter and the
Philosopher's Stone

J.K. Rowling · Fantasy · 1997

Harry Potter is an ordinary boy who discovers that he is actually a wizard. He joins Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he meets Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger.

Together, they experience magical adventures and discover a mystery involving the Philosopher's Stone.

My Review

I liked this book because it introduces the magical world of Hogwarts in a simple and interesting way. The friendship between Harry, Ron, and Hermione makes the story enjoyable.

02

BOOK TWO

Harry Potter and the
Chamber of Secrets

J.K. Rowling · Fantasy · 1998

Harry returns to Hogwarts for another school year, but strange things begin happening at the school. Students are mysteriously attacked.

Harry, Ron, and Hermione investigate the mystery and discover the secret behind the Chamber of Secrets.

My Review

I enjoyed this book because it combines mystery, friendship, and adventure. The story keeps the reader interested until the end.

03

BOOK THREE

Harry Potter and the
Prisoner of Azkaban

J.K. Rowling · Fantasy · 1999

The third book introduces Sirius Black, a prisoner who has escaped from Azkaban. Harry learns that Sirius is connected to his parents and their past.

During his third year at Hogwarts, Harry faces several mysteries and learns more about his family.

My Review

I liked this book because it has mystery and unexpected events. It also gives more information about Harry's past and makes the story more interesting.

04

BOOK FOUR

Harry Potter and the
Goblet of Fire

J.K. Rowling · Fantasy · 2000

In the fourth book, Hogwarts hosts the Triwizard Tournament. Harry unexpectedly becomes one of the contestants.

He has to complete several difficult tasks while dealing with new challenges. The wizarding world also begins to face greater danger.

My Review

I enjoyed this book because it contains more action and adventure. The tournament makes the story exciting and different from the earlier books.

05

BOOK FIVE

Harry Potter and the
Order of the Phoenix

J.K. Rowling · Fantasy · 2003

Harry begins his fifth year at Hogwarts while the wizarding world deals with the return of Lord Voldemort.

Harry and his friends form a group to practise defensive magic. They learn the importance of courage, friendship, and teamwork.

My Review

I liked this book because it shows the strength of friendship and teamwork. It also develops the main conflict of the series.
